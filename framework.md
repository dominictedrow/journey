Three Composite Inputs (replaces 5-factor checklist)
NOTE: These three composites encode all five original inputs but eliminate redundant confirmation steps. Each composite is self-contained—use the sources listed, not additional proxies.

Composite 1 — Monetary Conditions (highest weight)
Answers: Is global liquidity tightening or expanding?
Formula: Real rate = Fed funds rate minus CPI. Positive AND rising = genuinely restrictive.
Balance sheet: Fed balance sheet trend (H.4.1 weekly): QT drains liquidity even when nominal rate is unchanged.
Forward signal: Dot plot = FOMC forward guidance. Watch for pivot language and financial stability concerns.
Global M2: Global M2 (lynalden.com monthly): leads global equities by ~6 months. Contracting = risk-off bias.
Sources: federalreserve.gov (H.4.1, dot plot, statements) · fred.stlouisfed.org (M2SL, DGS10) · lynalden.com

Composite 2 — Credit & Risk Appetite
Answers: Are markets pricing risk-on or risk-off? Credit leads equities by ~2 weeks—this is your earliest warning system.
HY spread: HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2): CRITICAL. Widening >150bps in 6 weeks = reduce equities.
Yield curve: 2s/10s yield curve (T10Y2Y): inversion signals recession. Steepening in early cycle = bullish for banks.
VIX: VIX: <15 complacent, 15–25 normal, >35 panic. Spikes = potential buy; sustained >35 = structural stress.
MOVE: MOVE Index: bond market VIX. Sustained >120 precedes equity volatility.
Sources: fred.stlouisfed.org (BAMLH0A0HYM2, T10Y2Y, DFII10) · cboe.com/vix · daily check

Composite 3 — Dollar Regime & Cross-Asset
Answers: Is the USD strengthening or weakening? This single variable encodes EM risk, commodity direction, and global liquidity simultaneously.
DXY: DXY trend vs 50d and 200d MA. Rate of change > absolute level. Spike >5% in 15 trading days = risk-off.
Copper/gold: Copper/gold ratio: rising = growth > fear. Falling = fear > growth. The real-time growth barometer.
AUD/USD: AUD/USD as China demand proxy: strengthening = China healthy + global risk-on.
USD/JPY: USD/JPY carry signal: rapid fall >10% in 30 days = carry unwind = regime-change trigger.
Sources: tradingview.com (DXY, AUD/USD, USD/JPY, copper) · lme.com (copper) · weekly/daily check

TIER 2A — COUNTRY & REGION SCORING
Score major economies across four consolidated dimensions. Run monthly. Overweight top quartile (>16/20). Underweight/avoid bottom quartile (<10/20). The original six dimensions are consolidated—current account and fiscal are merged into one financial health dimension; political and geopolitical risk are merged into one risk dimension.

The 4-Dimension Scoring Model (1–5 per dimension)
Dimension 1 — Growth Trajectory
Real-time: Primary: PMI Composite above/below 50 and direction of change.
Trend: GDP growth vs trend. Is the economy surprising to the upside or downside vs consensus?
Sources: spglobal.com/pmi (40+ countries, 1st biz day of month) · data.oecd.org · tradingeconomics.com

Dimension 2 — Inflation & Central Bank Stance
CPI: Core CPI vs target, trend direction, and how far from the CB reaction threshold.
PPI lead: PPI leads CPI by 3–6 months. Falling PPI = early moderation signal.
CB stance: Is the CB tightening, holding, or easing? Does it have room to stimulate?
Sources: tradingeconomics.com (196 countries) · federalreserve.gov · ecb.europa.eu · boj.or.jp

Dimension 3 — Financial Health (Current Account + Fiscal)
Curr. account: Current account: Surplus countries (Germany, Japan, Korea) are creditors—currencies strengthen in stress. Deficit countries are vulnerable to DXY spikes.
Fiscal: Debt/GDP and trend. High and rising = constraint on future stimulus, eventual risk.
FX debt risk: Dollar-denominated debt + deteriorating current account = red flag combination.
Sources: imf.org/en/Publications/FM (Fiscal Monitor, April + October) · imf.org COFER (FX reserves)

Dimension 4 — Political Risk & Capital Flows
Political: Election calendar, policy credibility, CB independence, sanctions exposure.
ETF flows: Fund flows into country ETFs: rising inflows = accumulation. Outflows = exit.
Positioning: CFTC COT: extreme speculative positioning in either direction is a contrarian signal.
Sources: cfr.org/global-conflict-tracker · etf.com · cftc.gov/COT (Fridays) · pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar

MONITORING ROUTINE
Streamlined from four cadences to three. Quarterly deep-dives are absorbed into the monthly routine. Total time commitment: ~75 minutes per week.

Daily Scan (~10 minutes)
Signal check only. No position changes unless a regime-change trigger is hit.
DXY direction vs prior day. Any move >1% = flag for weekly review.
VIX level: crossing 15, 25, or 30 thresholds is significant.
HY credit spreads vs prior day. Widening >20bps = elevated attention.
Bitcoin ETF daily flows (farside.co.uk). Note direction and size.
FT or Bloomberg headlines. Flag geopolitical developments.
Check open positions against stops. No action unless a trigger is hit.

Weekly Review (~60 minutes — Sunday evening)
Full Tier 1 re-run. This is the core rhythm of the framework.
Run all three Composite inputs: monetary conditions, credit/risk, dollar/cross-asset. Classify current regime.
Crypto: BTC.D trend, ETH/BTC ratio, Fear & Greed, MVRV trajectory, ETF flow 7-day trend.
Review any central bank speeches, data releases, or geopolitical events from the past week.
Check CFTC COT for any new extreme positioning readings (contrarian signal).
Earnings surprises and analyst revisions for overweight positions.
Review open interest and funding rates (coinglass.com).

Monthly Deep-Dive (~2 hours — first weekend of month)
Full top-down re-run plus portfolio maintenance. Incorporates what was previously a separate quarterly routine.
Re-score all countries across 4 dimensions. Note any that crossed the 16 or 10 threshold.
Adjust sector overweights based on updated regime and country scores.
Re-run 4-factor checklist on all held positions and new candidates in overweight sectors.
Check sector and country valuations vs historical norms (starcapital.de, Damodaran annual in January).
Plot MVRV Z-Score trajectory. If approaching 5–6, begin reducing crypto allocation.
Full IMF WEO review when published (April + October). Update country scorecards with revised forecasts.
Portfolio attribution: which positions worked? Which thesis was wrong? Log outcomes.
Scenario planning: identify top 3 tail risks for the next month. Map each to regime-change triggers.

GLOSSARY OF KEY TERMS
Essential acronyms only. Full definitions available in original framework documentation.

TERM
MEANING
BTC.D
Bitcoin Dominance — BTC market cap as % of total crypto. Rising = early cycle, capital in BTC. Falling = altcoin season.
CAPE
Cyclically Adjusted P/E (Shiller P/E) — 10-year inflation-adjusted earnings multiple. Used for country valuation comparison.
COT
Commitments of Traders — CFTC weekly futures positioning report. Extreme readings = contrarian signals.
DXY
US Dollar Index — USD vs basket of 6 major currencies. Strong DXY = EM stress, commodity pressure.
EMBI
Emerging Market Bond Index — EM sovereign spread over US Treasuries. Widens with risk-off or DXY strength.
FCF
Free Cash Flow — cash after capex. Quality indicator; self-funding companies carry less credit risk.
HY / OAS
High Yield / Option-Adjusted Spread — junk bond yield premium over Treasuries. Leads equities by ~2 weeks.
MVRV
Market Value to Realized Value — Bitcoin on-chain ratio. Z-Score >7 = bubble. <0 = deep value.
QE / QT
Quantitative Easing / Tightening — CB balance sheet expansion (injects liquidity) vs contraction (drains).
ROIC
Return on Invested Capital — ROIC > WACC means a company is creating value, not destroying it.
TIPS / DFII10
Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities — 10yr real yield is the hurdle rate for gold and growth stocks.
VIX
CBOE Volatility Index — S&P 500 implied 30-day volatility. <15 complacent, >35 panic.
YCC
Yield Curve Control (BoJ) — caps 10yr JGB yield. Forced unlimited bond buying to defend the target.
