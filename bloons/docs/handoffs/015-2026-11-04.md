## Decisions made
- Fixed-position matching (box ± margin) instead of searching the whole frame. The UI is anchored to the window, so this is fast (7 ms for all 14) and avoids false hits elsewhere.
- FF on/off by colour probe: shape can't separate them, and normalized matching ignores brightness.
- Test frames as JPEG: a quarter of the size, and lossless templates against slightly noisy frames is a fairer test.
