## Decisions made
- The bar read feeds the existing clock estimates instead of replacing them. Guards, `abilities` and `observe()` keep one code path, and the estimate covers frames the reader can't see.
- The ring arc gives the fraction, not icon darkness: it's linear in time (residual ≤ 0.017) and doesn't depend on each icon's art.
- The ring, not the crop diff, confirms a use when readable: a live false "used" came from an icon coming off cooldown.
