## Decisions made
- Capture via CGWindowListCreateImage instead of mss: it works while the game is covered and gives 2× resolution.
- No modifier events at all, and `refocus()` is the recovery. Why: any synthetic modifier key-up made the game ignore plain keys.
- The game must be the active app for input; `ensure_front()` handles that before every input call.
