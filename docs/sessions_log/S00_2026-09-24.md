Learning notes:
1. **Why factorized (slice-then-study) attention:** full joint attention over one study is ~23k tokens → ~1 GB of attention scores
   per head per layer. Per-slice attention is ~256 tokens, and the study level is ~100 window tokens.
2. **Why MAE makes "from scratch" viable:** the ViT has no built-in locality bias, so it needs lots of data. MAE turns our
   ~800k unlabeled slices into a training signal (reconstruct the 75% of patches that are hidden) before it ever sees a label.
3. **Why the 58 gold studies are a weak ruler:** with 5 positives a single-finding AUC of 0.80 has a 95% CI of about 0.56–1.00, so
   only large or consistent improvements can be trusted on them.
