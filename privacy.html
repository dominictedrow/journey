Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Summary: Instana is a local desktop application. Your Instagram data never leaves your device except to communicate directly with Instagram's official API and (optionally) xAI's API for video classification. We do not operate servers, collect analytics, or share your data with any third party for advertising.

1. Who We Are

Instana is an independent desktop application developed by Dominic Tedrow (dominictedrow@gmail.com). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc. or Instagram.

2. What Data We Access

Instana requests the following Instagram permissions through the official Instagram Graph API:

  • instagram_basic — your Instagram account profile and media list
  • instagram_manage_insights — engagement metrics (plays, likes, comments, shares, saves) for your own content

We only access data for accounts you explicitly connect inside the app.

3. How We Store Data

All data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database at ~/Library/Application Support/Instana/instana.db (macOS). Your Instagram access token is stored in your macOS Keychain using system-level encryption. No data is transmitted to any server operated by Instana.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Instagram Graph API (Meta) — used to fetch your media and insights. Subject to Meta's Platform Policy.
  • xAI Grok API — video frames from your reels are sent to xAI's API solely for structural video classification (e.g., detecting hooks, text overlays, scene changes). No account credentials or personal identifiers are included. Subject to xAI's Privacy Policy. You can disable this feature in Settings.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect crash reports, analytics, or usage telemetry
  • We do not store or transmit your Instagram password
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers
  • We do not retain any data on external servers

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data exists only in the local database on your device. To delete all data, remove the application and delete the database file. You can also disconnect any account from within the app, which removes that account's tokens from your Keychain and its records from the local database.

7. Instagram Token Handling

Instana exchanges short-lived Instagram tokens for 60-day long-lived tokens using the official ig_exchange_token endpoint, and refreshes them automatically before expiry. Tokens are stored exclusively in your macOS Keychain and are never written to disk in plaintext.

8. Children's Privacy

Instana is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
