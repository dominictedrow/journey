## Corrections and scope caveats
- #22 points to an unrelated BIS market-liquidity publication. Use Evans and Lyons, *Order Flow and Exchange Rate Dynamics*, *Journal of Political Economy* 110(1), DOI https://doi.org/10.1086/324391 . The original paper concerns contemporaneous FX order flow and historical DEM/USD, not a causal trading rule for modern retail EUR/USD.
- #21 redirects to a paper titled *Interday Cross-Sectional Momentum: Global Evidence and Determinants*, not the exact supplied subtitle; record citation metadata after full-text verification.
- #12-13 MSCI previews are accessible but full reports request user information; do not bypass or claim full-paper review.
- #19-21 SSRN delivery links are not reliable full-text sources in this environment; verify authoritative abstracts, access and versions before quoting methods.
- All cited portfolio backtests reflect different sample periods, asset universes, turnover, margin and costs than retail MES/EURUSD. Reproduce from first principles.
- AQR datasets may serve long-horizon falsification/validation, **not** a substitute for exchange-grade intraday data.
